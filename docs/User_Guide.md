## 💡 Query Best Practices

- **Profile Queries**: Ask `"What is my employee ID?"`, `"What is my name?"`, or `"What department am I in?"`.
- **Requisition Queries**: Specify filters like status (`approved`, `pending`), description keywords, or date ranges (`April month requisition`, `this quarter`).
- **Follow-up Queries**: Use relative pronouns naturally (`"Who approved it?"`, `"What is its requested value?"`, `"Previous one"`).
- **Pagination Navigation**: Click the **`[← Previous]`** and **`[Next →]`** buttons below result cards, or type `"Next page"` / `"Page 2"`.
